Case Study 01 · IT Services & Digital Media

Scaling Digital Self-Publishing From Chaos to Conveyor Belt

The Challenge: Multidisciplinary teams — editors, designers, formatters, marketers — were coordinating over scattered chats and missed deadlines, publishing content globally across multiple e-book platforms with zero standard process.

The Action: As Project Manager / Senior Executive, we implemented standardized workflows for manuscript formatting, keyword SEO optimization, design coordination and platform distribution — each stage with an owner, an SLA and a metric on a shared board.

The Result: A high-volume client portfolio of 150+ customers managed monthly, 15+ projects closed every month, and distribution expanded across 12 e-book platforms — without adding management headcount.

Case Study 02 · Textile & Apparel Manufacturing

Scrum Meets the Factory Floor: 20 Exports, Zero Defects

The Challenge: A complex multi-stage apparel manufacturing lifecycle — knitting, dyeing, cutting, stitching, finishing — running on verbal updates, while the company's digital presence was effectively invisible.

The Action: As Project Manager (Order & Digital Management), we coordinated production teams using Scrum practices: daily standups per stage, visual boards, QC gates and vendor procurement audits — and simultaneously built the corporate WordPress website from zero.

The Result: 20 major apparel export shipments delivered within 1.5 years with 100% adherence to international quality standards — and a digital front door that started bringing inbound B2B inquiries.

Case Study 03 · IT Consulting

Technical Governance That Made Velocity Visible & Predictable

The Challenge: Rapid, multi-faceted software and technical projects with no operational structure — scopes blurred, risks untracked, and leadership flying blind on budgets and progress.

The Action: As Project Coordinator, we partnered with executive leadership to define project scopes, perform risk analyses, decompose deliverables into actionable tasks and run Agile design sprints with weekly executive readouts.

The Result: Project velocity improved 41% while maintaining tight control over budgets, resources and deliverable quality — nine consecutive sprints delivered without a single budget overrun.
